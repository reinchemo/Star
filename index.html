FAQ

Questions? Answered.

How fast is the installation?

Most installations are completed the same day you order — often within a few hours of your call. We'll give you a precise time window when we confirm your booking.

What's included in the free installation?

Everything. The router, the dish, mounting hardware, cabling, professional alignment, and full testing. You don't pay a shilling for setup.

Do I really get unlimited data?

Yes. No caps, no throttling, no FUP games. Stream, download, work — as much as you want.

Is there a contract or lock-in period?

No. All plans are month-to-month. You can upgrade, downgrade, or pause anytime.

What if my area isn't covered yet?

Call us. We're expanding fast, and we add new neighbourhoods every week based on demand. Your location may be next.

How do I pay?

M-Pesa, bank transfer, or cash on installation. We'll send a reminder before your monthly renewal.
